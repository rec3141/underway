2 · What you did

Tick the instruments your team worked with. The operations from the bridge's event log are listed below; untick any that were not yours.

Rosette bottles — which columns on the rosette sheets are your team?

Team names are typed by hand on each sheet. Obvious slips (case, a stray digit, one letter off or two swapped) are merged under one name; hover a name to see the spellings it includes. Tick every name that is your team; the number is how many casts it drew water from.

Import your team's logsheet

Upload the spreadsheet you kept on board (.xlsx or .csv). Each row is matched to an operation, first by event label (AMD####-###), then by station and time or date, then by time and position. Matched operations are ticked below, and the sheet's columns can go into your tables.

Digitize logbook photos

Photograph a paper logbook page (any orientation). A vision model transcribes every table on it; each cell is coloured by the model's estimate of the odds it is right (its reading and its place in the table), from green (near certain) to red (a guess). Click any cell to correct it — a corrected cell turns green. Export a table as TSV, all pages as one XLSX (a sheet per table, same colours), or use a table as a logsheet to pick your operations and fill your report tables. Each page takes about 30 seconds to 2 minutes.

3 · Conditions at your stations

Written from the event log, the ship's underway record, the rosette sheets and the Canadian Ice Service charts. Each value says where it came from; anything not recorded is said to be missing, never filled in.

A conditions table (one row per operation) can be added in step 4 with any of the conditions columns.

4 · Tables

Each table has one row per operation, per bottle or per logsheet row. Columns can come from any linked table: a bottle row can show its station's air temperature, a logsheet row its bottom depth.

5 · Figures

Photos and your own plots are best added in Word after downloading.

6 · Your text

Paragraphs separated by a blank line become separate paragraphs in Word. The generated narrative, tables and figures follow your text in the section you chose.

When you download, save the file as Cruise Report_TeamX.docx in \\10.0.0.10\share\2026\Leg X\Cruise Report before the end of your leg, and please fill in the Amundsen user survey.
