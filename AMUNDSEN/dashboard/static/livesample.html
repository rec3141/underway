    TSV columns & interpretation

    The selected-leg download lists each archived rosette bottle firing. Put 1 in sampled for bottles already sampled; leave others blank or enter 0. For a custom rosette TSV, use cast and bottle; include leg when cast numbers repeat. A measured pressure (dbar) can replace bottle. For flow-through samples, set source to flow and provide time (ISO 8601 with timezone), temperature (°C), and salinity (practical salinity). Leave unrelated cells empty. Unmatched marked rows are reported below.

    Recommendations are experimental guidance. Targets are chosen from water already observed during the downcast, for collection on the upcast. The reference population and sample coverage depend on the selected legs and available properties.
