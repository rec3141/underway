# The Photos tab

The **Photos** tab is the ship's photo album. It shows the photographs that
people aboard have added: the ice, the wildlife, the work on deck, the ship
itself. Each photo is placed on the map where and when it was taken, and has a
short description.

You can add your own photos, from the ship's shared drive or straight from
your phone or laptop.

!!! note "Aboard only"
    The photos are kept aboard. The public web copy of the dashboard has no
    **Photos** tab, and adding photos works only on the ship's network.

## Opening the gallery

1. Click **Photos** in the row of tabs at the top.
2. The right-hand side of the page shows **/Share Photos**, with two buttons:
   **Gallery** and **Submit**. **Gallery** is chosen.

"/Share" is the name of the ship's shared network drive, where everyone puts
their files. The gallery shows the photos that have been added from it.

![The gallery: a card for each photo, newest first](img/13-gallery.webp)

The photos are shown as cards, newest first. Each card has:

- the photo;
- the first sentence of its description, for example "A polar bear wanders
  across a vast snow-covered expanse…";
- the photographer and the date, for example **Clément Soriot · 2026-09-27**.

Scroll down the pane to see more. Below the photos, **Observations without a
picture** lists the journal entries that someone wrote by hand.

!!! tip
    A number on the **Photos** tab, for example **Photos 12**, says how many
    new photos have arrived since you last looked at the gallery in this
    browser. It goes away when you open the gallery.

The **Photos** tab is part of the wiki: the row of buttons above the gallery
(**‹ Back**, **Home**, **Explore**, the search box and the menu) belongs to the
wiki and is explained in [The Wiki tab](wiki.html). In the wiki's **Browse…**
menu, **/Share Photos** (under **Images**) brings you back here.

## Viewing a photo

1. Click a card in the gallery.
2. The photo's own page opens.

![A photo's page: the subject, the date and position, the photographer, and the photo with its navigation](img/13-photo.webp)

At the top of the page:

- The **title** is what the photo shows, for example ***Ursus maritimus*
  polar bear**. A scientific name is in italics, with its common name after
  it.
- The coloured dot and word give its **domain**, the field of nature it
  belongs to, for example **Biology**, **Sea ice** or **Meteorology**, or
  **Other** for people and the ship.
- The **date** it was taken.
- Its **position**, for example **75.587, -93.769**. Click it, or **map ↗**,
  to show that spot on the map.
- **BY** names the photographer and the ship.

Then the photo, with a bar above it:

- **← Gallery** goes back to the gallery.
- **17 / 500** says which photo this is, of how many.
- **‹** and **›** go to the previous and the next photo. The **←** and **→**
  keys on your keyboard do the same.

Below the photo are more details: what was observed, the method
(**camera**), the origin (**the ship's own journal**), and the full
description.

The descriptions are written by a computer model that looks at each photo
when it is added. They are usually right, but they are guesses. The model
also picks the subject and the domain.

### The slideshow

1. Click the photo itself.
2. It fills the whole screen.
3. Click **‹** and **›** at the sides, or press **←** and **→**, to move
   through the photos. The line at the bottom says, for example,
   **17 / 500 · polar bear · Clément Soriot**.
4. Click **×** at the top, or press **Esc**, to close the slideshow. The page
   shows the photo you stopped on.

### Flagging a photo

The small flag **⚑** after the title marks a page for review, for example a
wrong description or a photo that should not be shown. See
[Flagging a page for review](wiki.html#flagging-a-page-for-review).

## Photos on the map

The photos are also on the map beside the tab, when the **Photos** layer pill
above the map is on (dark blue). Click the pill to turn it on or off.

![The map with the Photos layer on: camera symbols for the daily timelapses, and coloured dots with a pale ring for the photos](img/13-map-photos.webp)

The layer shows two things:

- **A coloured dot with a pale ring** is a photo from the gallery. The dot's
  colour is its domain (the same colour as on its page). Hold the mouse over
  it to see what it shows, when and by whom. Click it to open its page.
- **A small camera symbol** is the day's **timelapse** from one of the ship's
  cameras: a short film of a whole day, placed where the ship was. Click it to
  play the film in a box over the map. In the box, **‹** and **›** go to the
  day before and after, and **✕** closes it.

The ring around a photo means it is the ship's own: it comes from the
journal, not from the wiki's history.

For more about the map, see [The map](map.html).

## Adding your own photos

You can add photos in two ways:

- **From the /Share folder**: the photos are already on the ship's shared
  drive, for example in your team's folder.
- **From this device**: the photos are on your phone, tablet or laptop. They
  are first copied to the shared drive, then added.

Either way, the ship's server then reads each photo: it finds when it was
taken, places it on the ship's track at that time, writes its description,
and adds it to the gallery. This is called an **import**.

!!! note "Aboard only"
    Importing needs the ship's server and its shared drive. It works only
    aboard.

### Adding photos from the /Share folder

1. On the **Photos** tab, click **Submit**.
2. **Import from:** shows two buttons. Click **/Share folder**.
3. Below it is a folder browser. The line at the top, for example
   **Share › Science › Leg 3**, is where you are; click a name in it to go
   back up. Click a folder (📁) to open it. The number after a folder is how
   many photos it holds. The photos in the open folder are shown as small
   pictures.
4. Open the folder that holds your photos. Everything in the open folder,
   and in its subfolders, will be added.
5. Fill in the form **Add to the journal**:

    ![The form for adding photos to the journal](img/13-submit.webp)

    - **Name**: the photographer, as the credit should read.
    - **Organisation**: your university or institute (optional).
    - **Email**: kept private to the ship; it is not shown in the gallery.
    - **Licence**: how others may use your photos.
    - **Time zone**: the clock your camera was set to (see below).

6. Tick **Keep importing from this /Share folder** if you will keep adding
   photos to this folder. New photos put there are then added every ten
   minutes, with the same credit and licence.
7. Click **Add to journal · 51 photos** (the button names how many). Until
   you open a folder it reads **Choose a folder** and cannot be clicked.

The licences in the **Licence** menu are:

| Licence | What it allows |
|---|---|
| **With credit** | reuse, as long as the photographer is credited |
| **CC BY 4.0** | any reuse, with credit |
| **CC BY-SA 4.0** | reuse with credit, under the same licence |
| **CC BY-NC 4.0** | reuse with credit, not for profit |
| **CC0** | no rights reserved |
| **Open access** | the ship's own pictures, free to use |
| **Rights reserved** | shown on the ship's wiki, not for reuse |

Open **Licence & time zone** under the form to read what the chosen licence
means.

The **Time zone** is used only when a photo does not record its own:

- **Camera / ship** (the usual choice): the time zone the camera wrote into
  the photo, or else ship time.
- **Ship (Eastern)**: ship time.
- **UTC**, or a fixed offset such as **UTC−05**: if you know your camera was
  set that way.

A wrong time zone puts a photo at the wrong place on the track, by hours of
sailing.

### Adding photos from your phone or laptop

1. On the **Photos** tab, click **Submit**, then **This device**.
2. In the folder browser at the top, open the /Share folder where the photos
   should go, for example your team's folder. A new subfolder is made in it
   for this upload.

    ![Upload photos from this device](img/13-submit-device.webp)

3. In **Folder name**, type a name for the new subfolder, or keep
   **Phone photos**.
4. Under **Photos**, choose the photos on your device.
5. The page says, for example, **12 photos selected · 48.3 MiB. Ready to
   upload.** Click **Upload Selected Photos**.
6. Keep the page open, and keep your phone awake, while the bar fills.
7. When it says **✓ 12 photos saved to the share**, click **Next: add to
   journal ↓**. The form **Add to the journal** appears, for the new folder.
8. Fill it in as above and click **Add to journal**.

Open **File limits** to see what can be uploaded: **JPEG, PNG or WebP · 300
photos · 64 MiB each · 2 GiB total**. iPhone HEIC photos and videos cannot be
uploaded; set the phone to save "Most Compatible" (JPEG) photos, or export
them as JPEG first.

If the connection drops, the page says how many were saved. Click
**Retry Remaining Photos**: the ones already saved are not sent again.
**New selection** or **Upload more photos** starts over.

Your original files are never changed.

### Following an import

After you click **Add to journal**, a box shows the import's progress:
**Importing…**, a bar, and what it is doing. When it ends, it reads **Import
finished** and, for example, **49/51 added · 1 skipped · 1 failed**.

- **View results** (or **Details** while it runs) opens a page listing every
  photo: its new description, subject, date and position, its tags, and
  whether it was **added**, **skipped** or **failed**, with the reason.
- A photo already in the journal is **skipped**. You can import the same
  folder again safely.
- A photo that **failed** is skipped in later imports until the file
  changes.

Below the form:

- **Watched folders** lists the folders that are imported every ten minutes,
  with who set them up and how many photos they have added. Click **Stop** to
  stop watching one.
- **Imports Status** lists recent imports. Click one to see its results.

The new photos appear in the gallery and on the map once the import is
finished.

## In short

- **Photos** opens the gallery of the ship's photographs. Click one for its
  page; click the photo for a full-screen slideshow.
- Turn on the **Photos** layer to see the photos and the daily camera
  timelapses on the map.
- **Submit** adds photos from the /Share folder or from your device; choose a
  credit, a licence and the camera's time zone.
- All of this works aboard only.
