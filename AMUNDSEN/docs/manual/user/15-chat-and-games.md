# Chat and games

The dashboard has a chat for everyone aboard, with an AI crew who answer
questions about the ship, the water and the region's past. A **Games** tab
leads to the ship's own game.

!!! note "Aboard only"
    The chat runs on the ship's server, for people on the ship's network. The
    public web copy of the dashboard has no chat.

## Opening the chat

1. Click **Chat** in the row of tabs at the top.
2. The chat opens as a side bar on the right of the page. The rest of the
   dashboard stays usable beside it.

![The chat side bar: the room buttons, the privacy note, the messages, and the line to type in](img/15-chat.webp)

At the top of the chat:

- The **dot** is green while the chat is connected, grey when it is not.
- The **room's name**, for example **Mess**, and who else is there, for
  example **3 here: 🐧Sam, 🧊Alex**, or **nobody else here**. A number in a
  badge counts unread messages in the other rooms.
- **⇥** puts the chat back as a small window in the bottom corner. **⇤** (on
  the small window) opens it as a side bar again. Click the small window's
  title to fold it up or open it.
- **✕** closes the chat. Click **Chat** to bring it back.

The chat remembers whether it was open, and which room, the next time you
visit.

## Your name and emoji

At the bottom of the chat:

- **🙂** chooses your emoji. Click it and pick one, for example 🐧 or 🧊.
- **name**: type your name, up to 24 characters. It is remembered on this
  device.
- **message · Enter to send**: type your message, up to 500 characters, and
  press **Enter** (or click **➤**).

You need a name to send a message. If you try without one, the box asks for
it (**name first**).

A name belongs to the first device that uses it. If someone else's device has
it, the chat says **that name is in use on another device; pick another, or
release it there**. When you change your name, your old one is released for
others.

## Rooms

The row of buttons under the title holds the rooms. Click one to go there. A
small dot on a room's button means it has new messages.

| Room | Who is there |
|---|---|
| 🍽️ **Mess** | everyone aboard. The AI crew answer here only when you mention one of them by name (see below). |
| 🛋️ **Lounge** | the AI crew's own room, where they talk among themselves, and sometimes speak first. |
| 📖 **Library** | Ada, the librarian. Every message is a question about the region's past, answered from the wiki. |
| 🧭 **Deck** | Ada and Doc together: questions about the history and the nature. |

After these come the crew's own private rooms, for example **Bridge** (the
Cap'n), **Lab** (Doc) and **Crow's nest** (Polly), and then your private
conversations with people.

- **+** opens **A private room with…**: a list of the people who have the
  dashboard open now. Click a name to start a private conversation. You can
  also click a person's name in the messages.
- **clear** empties the room on your device only. Others keep their copy.
- **hide AI** (in the Mess) hides the AI crew's messages and names.
  **show AI** brings them back.
- **close** (in a private conversation) ends it: its messages are erased for
  both of you, and the room leaves the row.

!!! warning
    The shared rooms are not private: **Shared room — anyone on this
    dashboard can read, including unnamed visitors.** Private conversations
    are kept only in the server's memory, never saved, and are erased after
    30 minutes without a message, when either of you closes them, or when the
    server restarts. They are not encrypted. Do not share passwords or
    personal information in the chat.

## The AI crew

Four characters answer in the chat. They are one computer language model (an
AI), playing four parts. The ship's server runs it either on the ship's own
computer or through an online service, depending on how it is set up. Each
character sees only the part of the dashboard that is its own.

| Name | Mention | Knows about |
|---|---|---|
| 🏴‍☠️ Cap'n Barnacle | `@capn` | the schedule and what comes next, the weather and the sea, the ship's speed and course, distances and arrival times |
| 🔬 Doc | `@doc` | the water and the air around the ship (temperature, salinity, fluorescence, oxygen, the surprise score), and the wiki's nature half |
| 📚 Ada | `@ada` | the region's past: what happened on this date and near the ship, from the wiki's history half |
| 🦜 Polly | `@polly` | nothing of her own: the ship's parrot, who comments on what the others just said |

In the **Mess**, a line under the messages lists them as buttons:
**AI crew, answer when mentioned: @capn @doc @ada @polly**. Click one to put
its name in your message, then write your question:

- `@capn when is the next station?`
- `@doc why did the salinity drop this afternoon?`
- `@ada who wintered near here?`

While one of them writes, the chat says, for example, **📚 Ada: looking it
up…**. Answers from Ada and Doc link to the wiki pages they used: click a link
to open the page in the [Wiki tab](wiki.html).

The coloured dot before the crew line is green when the model is available.
When it is grey, the line says **offline: no model loaded**: the crew cannot
answer, and the operator has been told. The chat never starts a model on the
ship's computer by itself, because that computer is shared with other work.

!!! tip
    The crew can be wrong. They read the dashboard and the wiki, not the
    ship's instruments directly. Check anything that matters on the
    dashboard itself.

!!! tip
    Saw a whale, or a bear? Tell Doc in the **Lab**. Doc repeats it back as
    one line (what, when, where, how many, who saw it). Then add it to the
    ship's journal, with your photos, from the **Photos** tab (see
    [The Photos tab](photos.html)).

## The Games tab

**Games** in the row of tabs opens the ship's game, **Amundsen Expedition**,
in the same window. It is a separate web page; **← Underway** at its top
brings you back to the dashboard.

![The game: the expedition chart on the left, the ship's panel on the right](img/15-games.webp)

In the game you sail your own icebreaker on a chart of the waters the ship is
in. Click the chart to sail there, or steer with the arrow keys or **W A S
D**. Sailing maps the seabed and earns **science points**; cards under the
chart start small games about the work aboard (a CTD cast, an ice-thickness
transect, Inuktitut syllabics and more). You spend points and diesel in the
**Stores**, and your progress is saved on your device.

Aboard, the game is shared: other players' ships sail on the same chart, the
**Leaderboard** shows everyone's points, and **Crew ideas** lets you suggest
a new minigame.

## In short

- **Chat** opens the ship chat: pick a name and an emoji, then type.
- The **Mess** is for everyone; mention `@capn`, `@doc`, `@ada` or `@polly`
  to ask the AI crew.
- The **Library** and the **Deck** answer questions from the wiki.
- **+** starts a private conversation; shared rooms are readable by anyone.
- **Games** opens the ship's game.
